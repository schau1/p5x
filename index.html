Instruction:

If you're using this, I assume you care enough to have the rotations down correctly. For example, Makoto should always have 4 moon stacks before he uses S3, so the calculator assumes he has max stacks. If you're not sure, his rotation is S1->S3->S1->S3, with someone buffing him every single turn. This tool only calculates damage for DPS that scales with ATK (Assassins and Sweepers but not Fox). In the future, I may expand it.
